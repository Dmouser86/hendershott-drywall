Proper drywall finishing cannot be rushed without risking shrinkage cracks. For smaller patches, we can often use rapid-setting "hot mud" compounds to finish faster, while whole-room taping and Level 5 skim coats require proper drying intervals between the tape coat, fill coat, and final finish coat.
